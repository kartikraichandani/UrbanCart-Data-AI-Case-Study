**To:** CMO  |  **From:** Growth Analytics  |  **Re:** Cutting paid spend by 20%

**Decision.** Cut ₹1,73,600 (20%) from the ₹8,68,000 Q2 paid-spend baseline: Meta Ads −₹1,31,000 (−38.6%) and Affiliate −₹42,600 (−25.1%). Hold Google Ads and Influencer flat. Check: ₹1,31,000 + ₹42,600 = ₹1,73,600 = 20% × ₹8,68,000.

**Why.**
- **Meta Ads:** Q2 spend rose 63.0% over Q1 (₹2,08,000 to ₹3,39,000) for only 17 extra customers (+5.2%). CAC climbed from ₹491 (Jan) to ₹1,115 (Jun); LTV:CAC is 0.62. We cut back to Q1 spend.
- **Affiliate:** worst return (LTV:CAC 0.42), only 8.1% repeat buyers, ₹957 revenue per customer.
- **Influencer:** highest CAC (₹967) but best revenue per customer (₹2,858) and 73.0% repeat. Not cut: CAC alone misleads.

**Protect: Google Ads.** The only channel whose LTV exceeds CAC (1.14), with stable CAC (+3.2%) and 57.7% repeat.

**Expected impact.** Roughly 82–199 fewer paid customers a quarter (7.5–18.2%), yet each lost customer earned less than it cost; net margin gain ₹88,117 even in the pessimistic case.

**Biggest risk.** Meta's CAC rise coincides with higher spend: association, not proof of saturation. LTV covers six months only and samples are modest, so volume could fall further.

**Monitor weekly:** Meta CAC and new customers, Organic signups (halo effect), Affiliate repeat rate, total new customers. If total paid customers fall over 18.2% below Q2 pace, revisit the Meta cut.
